Who is responsible and scope

Period Paws is a personal period and cycle tracker developed by Mustafa Nuri Pekdemir (MNPekdemir). This policy covers the iOS app, its widget and this information website. Contact: mnpekdemir.apps@gmail.com.

Period Paws requires no account. It does not send your health journal to the developer and has no Period Paws server or cloud sync storing it. There are no advertising, behavioral analytics or ad tracking SDKs. Your data is not sold or shared for advertising.

The app was previously named Luna. Its bundle identifiers, backup file type and existing data storage remain compatible after the rename.

Information stored on your device

Information you enter is processed on your device for the features you choose: optional name, period start/end dates, bleeding, pain, symptoms, discharge, temperature, LH tests, sexual activity and contraception, notes; cycle settings, pregnancy/breastfeeding/hormonal contraception preferences affecting predictions, notifications, lock and appearance settings.

The current interface does not request mood, weight, water or sleep input. Existing records created in earlier versions may retain those fields in storage, exports and backups to prevent data loss. Optional details, including your name, can be left blank. Period Paws does not read HealthKit data or request location, contacts, camera or microphone access.

Storage and device protection

The main journal is stored in the app’s private area using iOS file protection and is excluded from automatic device backups. Period Paws does not provide an automatic cloud backup. You must create your own encrypted backup before switching devices.

Optional app lock uses iOS authentication with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Period Paws does not receive biometric templates or your passcode. Protect your device and exported files; no storage method guarantees absolute security.

Optional widget sharing

Showing dates in the widget is off by default. If you enable it in Period Paws settings, a small date summary is written to an on-device shared area used only by Period Paws and its widget extension: latest period start/end, cycle and period lengths, prediction/pause preferences, ovulation offset and last update date.

Your name, notes, sexual activity and symptom details are not shared with the widget. App lock does not hide the widget. People who can see your screen may see its dates. The widget file is excluded from automatic backup and uses iOS protection that permits widget access after the first device unlock.

Turning sharing off or erasing app records clears the shared dates and requests a widget refresh. iOS controls when cached widget views refresh. You can also remove the widget from your Home Screen.

Backups and exports

You choose whether to create a .lunabackup file, its password and its destination. Backups use AES-256-GCM encryption with a key derived using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, 600,000 iterations and a random salt. The backup password is not saved and there is no recovery key. We cannot unlock a backup if you forget its password. It contains journal data and relevant preferences; purchases are verified through your Apple account.

PDF and CSV exports are not encrypted and may contain sensitive health information and notes. Files you choose to send to iCloud, email or other services using Files or sharing are subject to those services’ rules. Period Paws does not send them automatically. You control the security and deletion of external copies. Confirming a restore replaces current journal records with the backup records.

Notifications and Apple services

If you grant permission, period reminders are scheduled locally on the device. Their text contains no health details; Period Paws has no remote notification server. You can turn reminders off in Period Paws or revoke permission in iOS Settings → Notifications → Period Paws.

Product information, purchases and purchase restoration may connect to Apple through StoreKit. Period Paws processes Apple product/transaction information on the device to verify theme access; it does not receive your card number or Apple account password. Apple processes data under its Privacy Policy. Operating system diagnostics and sharing settings are controlled by Apple; Period Paws includes no additional analytics or crash tracking SDK.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and voluntary attachments through Gmail. They are used to respond and resolve your request for as long as necessary, or as required by law. Unnecessary correspondence is deleted. Do not send health records, backup files, passwords or payment details. Redact personal information from screenshots.

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, advertising, forms, cookies or local storage code. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses for security when you visit; its Privacy Statement applies. GitHub Pages and Gmail may process information outside your country. Opening these pages does not send your app’s health journal.

Your controls and deletion

You can view, edit and delete individual records in Period Paws. Settings → “Tüm kayıtlarımı sil” deletes the main local records, removes pending local notifications and clears shared widget dates. Deleting the app removes local app data; copies you previously created as backups, PDFs, CSVs or emails remain until you delete them separately. iOS manages cached widget views; remove the widget if you do not want it visible.

We have no copy of your health journal and cannot remotely erase or recover it. For access, correction, deletion or other rights provided by applicable law concerning support correspondence, contact the support address. We request only information necessary to verify your request, never your health file. Your statutory rights remain unaffected.

Changes

We update this page and its date when features or data practices change. New optional sharing is accompanied by the relevant in-app explanation and permissions. This describes technical behavior; it is not a medical service or a certification of legal compliance.
