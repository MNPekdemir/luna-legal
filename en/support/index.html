You can include your Luna version, iOS version and steps that led to the issue. Do not send health records, backup files, device/backup passwords or payment details. Redact personal information from screenshots. This is not a medical advice or emergency service.

Calendar and future months

Use calendar arrows or the month picker to view up to 12 months ahead of today. Colored ranges are estimated dates. If a period is overdue, Luna keeps the first expected date rather than creating a new period record automatically. Record the actual start when your next period begins.

Pregnancy, breastfeeding or hormonal contraception settings may pause predictions. Fertility estimates may be hidden when history is irregular or uncertain. Predictions are not contraception or confirmation of ovulation.

Adding a widget

First enable “Widget’ta takip tarihlerini göster” in Luna → Ben. Long-press a blank area on your Home Screen → Edit/Add Widget → Luna → choose small or medium. Tap the widget to open the calendar. If information appears stale, open Luna; iOS controls refresh timing. App lock does not hide widget dates. You can turn sharing off or remove the widget.

Backups and device changes

Open Ben → “Şifreli yedek ve geri yükleme” and create a .lunabackup file using a password of at least 12 characters. Keep it outside the Luna folder. On the new device, select it in the same section, enter the password, review the preview and confirm replacement of current records. The password is not saved and cannot be recovered. Re-enable reminders after restore; the current device’s lock and widget sharing preferences are preserved.

Reminders and app lock

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Luna for permission. Focus and silent settings may affect presentation. No new reminder is scheduled when the expected date is in the past. App lock uses iOS Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode; do not share your passcode with support.

Themes and purchases

Pati is free. Puppy and rabbit themes can be previewed; the live App Store catalog is not yet active for real purchases. When sales are available, each theme is a separate one-time product. Purchased themes can be verified using “Satın alımları geri yükle” with the same Apple account. For refunds, use Apple Report a Problem.

Deleting data

Ben → “Tüm kayıtlarımı sil” deletes local records, removes pending local notifications and clears widget dates. Delete exported backups, PDFs and CSVs separately. iOS controls cached widget refresh; remove the widget if you do not want it visible. We hold no copy of your health file and cannot remotely erase or recover it.
