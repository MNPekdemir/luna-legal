Limits of predictions

Luna provides no diagnosis, treatment, contraception or emergency care. Future period, ovulation and fertile window dates are approximate calculations based on your records and selected settings, not confirmation of ovulation. Cycles can vary. Dates shown in future months do not establish that menstruation or fertility will occur on those dates.

Do not use Luna to prevent pregnancy, determine whether you are pregnant or make medical decisions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns. Reminders and widget updates depend on iOS permissions, device time and system conditions.

Records and backups

You control the accuracy of information you enter and can correct it. Records stay on your device; there is no automatic Luna cloud backup. Before deleting the app or changing devices, create an encrypted backup, store it outside the Luna folder and keep its password safe. We cannot recover forgotten backup passwords or deleted local data.

Restoring a backup replaces existing records after preview and confirmation. PDFs and CSVs are sharing formats and cannot be restored into Luna. If you enable widget sharing, dates appear on the Home Screen; app lock does not protect the widget.
