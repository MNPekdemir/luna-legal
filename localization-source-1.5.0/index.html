350How is it calculated?
351{{0}}. Ovulation is assumed {{1}} days before the expected menstrual period start. Fertility window is this day and the previous five days. premenstrual tracking window is {{2}} days. These are not personal measurements but variable assumptions.
352The calendar shows forward predictions assuming the same menstrual cycle length will continue for 12 months from today. Future menstrual cycles do not create real records. When a new menstrual period record is added, dates are recalculated; distant months are less certain. The expected first date in case of delay is preserved; notifications do not shift to hypothetical menstrual cycles. Missing past menstrual cycles are not filled.
